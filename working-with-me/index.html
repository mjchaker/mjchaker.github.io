The non-negotiables

01

The rate: $20 an hour, flat

Same rate for everyone, every subject, online or in person. I don't haggle — not because I'm precious about it, but because a fixed rate is the only one that's fair to the people who don't ask. It stays simple: an hour costs twenty dollars.

02

I teach. I don't do the work for you.

No completing graded assignments, no writing your lab report, no "just this once" the night before a due date. I will happily work the same kind of problem beside you until you can do yours alone — that's the entire point of hiring me.

03

Cancellation: 24 hours' notice

Life happens, and rescheduling is free — with a day's warning. A session cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice is a session I planned, prepared, and held the evening open for. Repeat no-shows end the arrangement.

04

Understanding is the exit condition

We don't stop at the right answer; we stop when you can explain it back to me simply. If you want someone to race through a problem set and call it studying, I'm the wrong tutor — and I'd rather say that here than in session.

The negotiables

05

When we meet

I work weekday evenings and weekends. Inside that window, scheduling is yours: weekly, twice weekly, or an intensive block before an exam.

06

Where we meet

Online or in person — whichever helps you learn. In-person location is by arrangement; online just needs a working mic and something to write on.

07

What we cover

Math, chemistry, physics — any high-school level, from patching old gaps to pushing ahead of the class. Session length is flexible too: an hour is standard, longer by arrangement.

08

How we work

Your textbook, your teacher's notation, your past tests. I adapt the method to the student, not the other way around.

Messages

09

Response time: within 24 hours on weekdays

Email is the reliable channel. Weekday messages get an answer within a day; weekends I reply when I can, without promising it. Tell me the subject, the level, and what's not clicking — we'll take it from there.

Sound fair? Then the rest is easy.
